# Buying a Gravel Bike

It's been 14 years since I bought a bike. A 2012 [Fuji Roubaix](https://99spokes.com/bikes/fuji/2012/roubaix-1.0) in white and black. Back then, a full Shimano 105 (save for the crank, and front derailleur) set me back $1,200. Today you'll spend 2.5x that for a similar build. I was shocked when I asked Claude for gravel bike recommendations, and it gave me options to select my price range, setting the floor at $2,800.

So this sent me down a rabbit hole re-aquanting myself with bicycles, groupsets, and brands. I've learned a fair bit, and this post includes some of the highlights so I need not look too far the next time I'm in the market.

## Why Gravel
My riding has changed. The Roubaix was bought for a specific purpose; I participated in the [Pan Ohio Hope Ride](https://pohr.org) 3 years in a row. Biking across an entire state is challenging. Doing it to raise money for folks battling cancer lowers the difficulty considerably. Every time things get bleak, your brain silently utters "man, this sucks. but you know what would suck more?" and you answer aloud "fighting cancer". Then you're flooded with names and faces of the scores of people you know who've done it. And you're stronger.

The POHR was great. I have a bond with my teammates 'The Cardinals' that'll last a lifetime. I recognize riders on the Towpath, I see POHR t-shirts throughout the state and it's nice being able to strike up a conversation with instant comradery.

But that was then. Now I've got three kids, usually two of them in the burley behind my bike when we ride. We stick to the [Towpath](https://www.ohioanderiecanalway.com/explore/the-towpath-trail/), and the stiff, road hungry Roubaix leaves a bit to be desired in the comfort department on rougher sections of the trails. I want something newer, something more comfortable, something I can still go fast with. Gravel ticks a lot of those boxes.

## Groupsets
Holy Moly are there a lot of options. Despite the cost of bikes increasing significantly, I do believe that the tech in $3k bikes today was top-of-the-line tour de france material a decade ago.
